### 1. TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31233v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-23
- **作者/机构**：Hani Alomari, Md Atabuzzaman, Chris Thomas
- **主题标签**：`RAG`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.CV, cs.LG

#### 一句话结论
TowerBridge通过轻度衰减双塔在困难负样本上更一致的方向，在基本保留模态间隙时提升检索，并固化为闭式权重编辑。

#### 核心内容
- CLIP双编码器存在模态间隙；失败常归因于困难负样本，但事后移除间隙会损害检索。
- 成对统计量定位双塔对困难负样本比匹配对更一致的方向，轻度衰减可提升检索且大体保留间隙。
- 权重空间编程让LLM代理写闭式权重编辑，无需反向传播；常见修正是输出投影，后提炼为TowerBridge。

#### 方法与数据
- TowerBridge是基于困难负样本margin一阶分析的闭式配方，从25,000个有标签对秒级计算，无需模型特定调参，不改变架构和推理成本。
- 21个CLIP检查点（含8个未见）上，COCO检索平均+16.8 RSUM、Flickr30k+6.6，ImageNet零样本下降≤0.5点；均降hubness，其他家族5个中4个提升。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：不改架构与推理成本即可提升检索，并限制零样本损失。
- **可复用点**：成对统计量定位有害方向，闭式权重编辑替代事后消除间隙。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未明确代码复现、任务泛化及失败条件；其他家族仅5个中4个提升。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
CLIP-style dual encoders map images and text through two separate towers into a shared embedding space, where the two modalities occupy distinct regions separated by a modality gap. Retrieval fails when wrong matches, or hard negatives, outrank the correct one, and the gap is the usual suspect. Yet removing it post hoc degrades retrieval on every CLIP checkpoint we test. We introduce a paired statistic that identifies directions where the two towers agree more on hard negatives than on matched pairs. Mildly attenuating these directions improves retrieval while largely preserving the gap. We arrive at this correction through weight-space programming: guided by the statistic, an LLM agent inspects both towers and writes closed-form weight edits without backpropagation. Free to edit any component, the agent improves COCO test retrieval on all 13 CLIP checkpoints and edits an output projection in 12 of the 13. We distill and refine its recurring correction into TowerBridge, a closed-form recipe motivated by a first-order analysis of the hard-negative margin. Computed in seconds from 25,000 labelled pairs, it requires no model-specific tuning and leaves the architecture and inference cost unchanged. TowerBridge improves COCO test retrieval by 16.8 RSUM and Flickr30k test retrieval by 6.6 on average across all 21 evaluated CLIP checkpoints, including 8 unseen by the agent, while limiting any drop in ImageNet zero-shot accuracy to 0.5 points. It also reduces hubness on all 21 checkpoints and improves retrieval on 4 of 5 dual encoders from other families.

### 2. TideSand: Performance-Accurate Sandbox Multiplexing for LLM Agents

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31118v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-23
- **作者/机构**：Shulu Li, Hanchen Li, Xiaokun Chen, Qiuyang Mang, Hanfei Chen, Jacky Kwok
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.OS

#### 一句话结论
TideSand 是面向性能敏感 LLM Agent 的沙箱系统，通过智能多路复用资源，在保持工具调用保真度的同时提升吞吐并降低评估误差。

#### 核心内容
- 采用智能体感知调度，在工具调用级别安排沙箱执行，避免资源竞争。
- 推测性换出：将等待推理的沙箱内存换出，并在下次工具调用前恢复。
- 选择性回滚：竞争时对选定沙箱设检查点，机器空闲时恢复。

#### 方法与数据
- 在 AlgoTune、Terminal-Bench 4.0、Terminal-Bench Science 上评估六个模型-工作负载对，与严格预留和超售基线比较。
- 相比严格预留吞吐提高 3.8-10 倍且保持保真度；相近吞吐下比超售基线保真度高 7-44%；AlgoTune 评估误差降低 19 倍。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：为保持工具调用保真度并提高资源利用率提供统计多路复用思路。
- **可复用点**：工具调用级调度、推理等待时换出、竞争时选择性回滚与空闲恢复。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未明确系统开销、负载泛化性与长期稳定性，需核查。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
We propose TideSand, a sandbox system that intelligently multiplexes resources for performance-sensitive LLM agents, such as auto research agents working on system measurement tasks. TideSand combines three key ideas: agent-aware scheduling, which schedules sandbox execution intelligently at the tool call level to avoid resource contention; speculative swapping, which offloads the memory of sandboxes waiting on inference and restores it before their next tool call; and selective rollback, which checkpoints selected sandboxes when contention occurs and restores them when machines are idle. We evaluate TideSand across six model-workload pairs on AlgoTune, Terminal-Bench 4.0, and Terminal-Bench Science. TideSand achieves 3.8-10x higher throughput than strict reservation while maintaining tool-call fidelity, or 7-44% higher fidelity than overcommit baselines at similar throughput. Moreover, TideSand reduces the evaluation error on Algotune by 19x through improved tool-call fidelity. We hope the work motivates future research to maintain tool call fidelity while keeping resource utilization high through statistical multiplexing.

</details>
