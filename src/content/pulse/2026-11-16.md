### 1. Agentic induction of common data elements and information extraction from sarcoma case report abstracts

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18506v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-13
- **作者/机构**：Daniel Reichenpfader, Kwaku Appiagyei, Manovah Nadarajah, Ahmed Reda Bahr, Irina Filchenko, Fabio Dennstädt
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.CL

#### 一句话结论
LLM代理可从肉瘤病例报告摘要生成并整合CDE候选，并尝试大规模证据关联抽取，但CDE质量与字段准确性仍需专家把关。

#### 核心内容
- 从24,588篇PubMed摘要提出72,909个候选CDE，整合后得1,570个。
- 保留至少50篇摘要支持的53个CDE；79.2%摘要对所有保留CDE处理成功（含“未提及”）。
- 人工审阅认可83.0%保留CDE相关性、67.9%清晰性、56.6%正确性。

#### 方法与数据
- 数据为24,588篇PubMed肉瘤病例报告摘要；流程含代理生成、整合、支持数筛选和证据关联抽取；100篇抽样中1,116个非空答案对的LLM-人工精确一致率94.9%（95%CI 93.0-96.4%）。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：支持“代理辅助模式起草+专家策展+大规模抽取”的路径。
- **可复用点**：候选CDE生成、整合、证据关联抽取及人工审阅评价流程。
- **局限/待核查**：CDE正确性认可率仅56.6%，错误集中于解剖和时间字段；摘要未明确其他验证。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Common data elements (CDEs) support clinical data comparability but are usually defined manually. We investigated whether large language model agents could derive a CDE schema from sarcoma case report abstracts and apply it at scale. An agent proposed 72,909 candidate CDEs from 24,588 PubMed abstracts; agentic consolidation yielded 1,570 CDEs. We retained 53 supported by at least 50 abstracts and attempted evidence-linked extraction across the corpus. Processing succeeded for all retained CDEs in 79.2% of abstracts, including responses indicating non-mention. Two human reviewers approved 83.0% of retained CDEs for relevance, 67.9% for clarity, and 56.6% for correctness. In 100 sampled abstracts, exact agreement with the value extracted by the LLM was 94.9% among 1,116 pairs with a non-empty answer (abstract-level bootstrap 95% CI, 93.0-96.4%). Errors concentrated in anatomical and temporal fields. These findings support agent-assisted schema drafting followed by expert curation before large-scale extraction.

</details>

### 2. From AI-Enabled Remote Patient Monitoring Toward Agentic Robotics: Embodiment, Governance, and Care Integration

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18484v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-13
- **作者/机构**：Ivana Ragusa, Lorenzo Guiducci, Filippo Cavallo
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.MA, cs.SE, eess.SY

#### 一句话结论
该综述指出，成熟代理式护理尚未出现；未来需发展嵌入护理生态、受监督且可审计可中断的代理机器人。

#### 核心内容
- AI远程监测正从被动数据收集转向能解释患者状态、协调护理并启动行动的代理AI。
- 多数系统仍由人主导，更强的决策—行动耦合与具身AI主要限于原型和试点。
- 医疗机器人将AI建议转为语音、移动、提示、身体协助、升级处置和护理协调，使具身、临床整合、监督与治理成为基本设计要求。

#### 方法与数据
- 采用结构化范围综述；摘要未明确数据库、检索时限、纳入文献数量等。
- 摘要未明确具体评价指标。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：提出代理机器人方向，强调监督、可审计工作流与明确AI治理。
- **可复用点**：具身、临床整合、人类监督、治理及可中断可审计工作流可作设计要点。
- **局限/待核查**：成熟代理护理未出现，强耦合与具身系统多为原型/试点，临床有效性待核查。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
AI-enabled remote patient monitoring is evolving beyond passive data collection toward agentic AI systems that interpret patient states, coordinate care, and initiate action across domestic and clinical settings. This structured scoping review finds that mature agentic care has not yet emerged: Most systems remain human led, while stronger decision-action coupling and embodied AI remain largely confined to prototypes and pilots. Healthcare robotics makes the transition from monitoring to action especially visible by translating AI-generated recommendations and decisions into speech, movement, prompting, physical assistance, escalation, and care coordination, making embodiment, clinical integration, human oversight, and governance fundamental design requirements. Further progress requires safe and interoperable care architectures in which intelligent components operate through clinically validated and accountable workflows. We propose agentic robotics as a future direction for supervised systems embedded in the care ecosystem, connecting AI agents and robots with clinicians, caregivers, and patients through auditable workflows that can be interrupted and remain subject to human oversight and explicit AI governance.

</details>
