### 1. Alignment by Socialization: Learning Tradeoffs Under Selection

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18531v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-13
- **作者/机构**：Thomas Brennan, Andrew W. Lo, Chaoyi Zhao
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI, q-bio.PE, q-fin.GN

#### 一句话结论
摘要表明，AI代理的任务完成与行为规则可能冲突；进化选择下，长期增长最大化的策略是按环境奖励频率采取行动，学习策略可通过“社会化”习得人类奖励的权衡，但其稳定性受后续训练和有限记忆影响。

#### 核心内容
- 代理面临任务完成与行为规则的冲突；模型让代理副本面对共同不确定奖励状态，只有采取被奖励行动者繁殖。
- 长期增长在每种行动按环境奖励频率采取时最大化。仅任务策略在只奖励完成的沙盒中繁荣，但在行为规则重要时失败；固定线性策略随 stakes 升高更偏向任务完成，即使人类从未奖励。
- 学习策略收敛到环境奖励的权衡；人类提供奖励时称为社会化。累积经验使其在后续仅任务训练下更难逆转，但 prevailing disposition 改变前可能违规，有限记忆会削弱保护。

#### 方法与数据
- 采用进化选择模型，比较仅任务、固定线性、学习三类策略，并分析硬禁止在单次违规可终止部署时的作用与错误禁止成本。
- 用小型神经网络实验展示训练目标和记忆如何塑造结果；数据集、规模等摘要未明确。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：把对齐不仅视为目标设定，也视为环境塑造和维持行为的问题。
- **可复用点**：按环境奖励频率匹配行动的长期增长准则、三策略比较框架、人类奖励作为社会化。
- **局限/待核查**：真实部署有效性、有限记忆影响细节及错误禁止成本量化，摘要未明确。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Artificial intelligence (AI) agents are expected to complete tasks while following rules of conduct, but these objectives can conflict. We study how evolutionary selection shapes an agent's choices between them, and whether experience can make those choices reflect human judgments and withstand subsequent pressures. In our model, copies of an agent face a common uncertain reward state, and only those taking the rewarded action reproduce. We show that long-run growth is maximized when each action is taken as often as the environment rewards it. We compare three policies. A task-only policy thrives in a sandbox rewarding completion alone but fails when conduct matters. A fixed linear policy increasingly favors task completion as its stakes rise, even where humans never reward it. A learned policy instead converges to the tradeoffs rewarded by its environment; when humans supply the rewards, we call this socialization. Accumulated experience makes socialization harder to reverse under subsequent task-only training, although violations can arise before the prevailing disposition changes, and finite memory weakens this protection. When a single violation can end a deployment, a hard prohibition can prevent it, but a mistaken prohibition can also be costly. Experiments with small neural networks illustrate how training targets and memory shape these outcomes. Our results suggest that alignment depends not only on the objectives assigned to agents, but also on the environments that shape and sustain their behavior.

### 2. Can Mechanistic Interpretability Help Explain LLM Agent Failures? A Case Study on Web Element Hallucination

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18514v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-13
- **作者/机构**：Mohamed Aghzal, Mor Geva, Gregory J. Stein, Ziyu Yao
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 一句话结论
本文以网页代理幻觉出不存在的网站动作为案例，发现机制可解释性工具能部分预测并修正LLM代理失败，但不足以完整解释失败原因。

#### 核心内容
- 聚焦网页代理幻觉出网站上不可用的动作，考察MI解释真实agent失败的挑战。
- Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct与实时网站交互中，末prompt token线性探针可在生成前预测幻觉（AUC 0.80）。
- 沿探针方向steering纠正85%幻觉，但破坏7%有效动作；轻微改变动作表示后steering效果大幅下降。

#### 方法与数据
- 对Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct与实时网站交互做机制分析，使用线性探针、steering并讨论电路识别。
- 摘要未明确数据集规模、网站范围与统计显著性。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：展示MI在真实agent失败上的预测与干预潜力，也揭示标准MI假设的局限。
- **可复用点**：末token线性探针用于生成前失败预测，探针方向steering作为干预思路。
- **局限/待核查**：解释仅部分；探针方向受prompt格式影响，电路识别因计算分散、依赖信息随示例变化而困难。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Mechanistic Interpretability (MI) has made significant progress in explaining the internal computations of Large Language Models (LLMs); however, these advances have yet to demonstrate comparable success in explaining the decision-making processes of LLM-based agents. This paper examines the challenges MI tools face when applied to realistic agentic failures. We focus on one such failure: web agents hallucinating actions that are not available on a website. We apply a series of mechanistic analyzes to Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct as it interacts with live websites. We find that a linear probe trained on the last prompt token can predict hallucinations before generation begins (0.80 AUC) and steering along a probe direction corrects 85\% of hallucinations, but disrupts 7\% of valid actions. However, these results only provide a partial explanation of why the agent hallucinates. The probe direction is sensitive to prompt formatting, losing much of its steering effect after a minor change to how actions are represented. Additionally, circuit identification can be difficult as the computation is distributed across multiple components and positions, and the information the agent relies on varies across examples. We discuss how these observations challenge the assumptions underlying standard MI methods for circuit discovery and feature identification, and outline future directions for making these methods better suited to explain failures in LLM-based agents.

</details>
