### 1. Flide: Accelerating Decision Inference with Exact State Forking

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42148v1)
- **日期**：2026-10-30
- **作者/机构**：Bobo Li, Jiajia Song, Hao Fei, Wynne Hsu, Mong Li Lee
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.CL

#### 一句话结论
Flide 是一款面向智能体决策的高效精确推理引擎，在保证数学等价性的同时实现了高达 9.5 倍的推理加速。

#### 核心内容
- 针对现有引擎前缀重计算开销大及输出概率失真问题，Flide 将决策请求的逻辑树结构与物理扁平 Token 流解耦。
- 提出 ForkAttention，通过 GPU 驻留索引处理标准注意力和循环 Gated DeltaNet 层，确保各分支计算与独立前向传播完全一致。
- 利用 GPU 元数据表达分支拓扑，支持紧凑预录图执行及智能体历史检查点恢复。

#### 方法与数据
- 在公开混合模型和决策模型上进行了广泛评测，具体模型细节与测试数据集摘要未明确。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：推理速度达 vLLM 和 SGLang 的 9.5 倍，且总变差距离最接近 float32 基准。
- **可复用点**：跨标准注意力与循环层的 ForkAttention 状态分支及拓扑索引机制。
- **局限/待核查**：支持的模型架构规模及复杂场景泛化性摘要未明确。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Decision models such as Jev have emerged as a highly promising paradigm for LLM agent systems, providing lightweight and reliable categorical reasoning for rapid System One execution. However, existing generation-oriented inference engines fail to support these workloads effectively, suffering from severe prefix recomputation overheads or distorted output probabilities. To address this challenge, we propose Flide, an efficient and exact inference engine that decouples the logical tree structure of a decision request from its physical execution within a single flattened token stream. Through ForkAttention, Flide navigates request dependencies using GPU-resident indices across both standard attention and recurrent Gated DeltaNet layers, guaranteeing in exact arithmetic that every query branch evaluates identically to an isolated forward pass. By expressing branch topologies directly in GPU-resident metadata, Flide accommodates diverse request mixtures with a compact set of pre-recorded execution graphs while enabling expanding agent histories to resume from checkpoints saved in earlier passes. Extensive evaluations on public hybrid and decision models demonstrate that Flide answers queries up to 9.5 times faster than vLLM and SGLang while staying closest to a float32 reference in total variation distance. By unifying physical execution throughput with mathematical exactness, Flide establishes a principled foundation for scalable, latency-critical decision intelligence in agent systems.

### 2. Quantum 3xplorer: AI-Native Engine for Next-Generation Quantum Materials Modeling

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42108v1)
- **日期**：2026-10-30
- **作者/机构**：Vsevolod Biryukov, Sergey V. Barabash, Pranab Kumar Das, Jack Deslippe, Mauro Del Ben, Felipe H. da Jornada
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cond-mat.mtrl-sci

#### 一句话结论
Quantum 3xplorer (Q3) 是由领域科学家指导、AI 智能体从零构建的 AI 原生量子模拟引擎，仅用数周便实现了高性能且支持 GPU 扩展的端到端平面波 DFT 计算。

#### 核心内容
- **开发范式革新**：科学家负责物理建模与验证，AI 智能体承担代码编写与优化，将底层科学软件数年的开发周期压缩至数周。
- **功能完备高效**：完整实现 PAW、DFT+U、色散校正及结构弛豫等功能，单核运行速度优于基准实现，并能在 GPU 超算上良好扩展。
- **分层可扩展架构**：将量子概念与数值表示解耦，输入输出均经模式验证且天然 AI-ready，易于泛化扩展至其他基组与理论。

#### 方法与数据
- **方法**：采用物理优先的分层架构设计，由 AI 编码智能体全流程从零编写平面波赝势 DFT 求解引擎。
- **数据**：使用代表性基准测试及 GPU 加速超算进行验证；具体基准测试集细节摘要未明确。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：展示了 AI 编写高性能底层量子计算软件的可行性，大幅加速新理论到代码落地的过程。
- **可复用点**：代码遵循 Apache 2.0 协议开源，提供标准化、AI 可直接读取的数据架构。
- **局限/待核查**：针对复杂真实材料体系的计算精度与大规模多卡并行效率仍待查验正文数据。

<summary>摘要</summary>
Quantum-mechanical approaches, such as density functional theory (DFT), underpin much of modern computational materials science and are the primary source of data for AI-driven discovery. Yet the established electronic-structure codes today represent decades-old software architectures, making new physical models slow and costly to implement, validate, and deploy on emerging hardware. We present Quantum 3xplorer (Q3, pronounced "Quantum Explorer"), an AI-native quantum simulation engine written from scratch by AI coding agents under the direction and supervision of domain scientists. As a first and demanding validation case, we implement plane-wave pseudopotential DFT end to end: norm-conserving, ultrasoft, and projector-augmented-wave formalisms, spin polarization, DFT+U, and dispersion corrections, together with forces, stresses, structural relaxation, and electronic-structure analysis. Q3 reproduces established reference implementations, runs faster on a single core in representative benchmarks, and scales across GPU-accelerated supercomputers. It was developed in weeks rather than years. Its layered, physics-first architecture separates abstract quantum-mechanical concepts from their numerical representations, making its framework extendable beyond plane waves to other basis sets and levels of theory. Its inputs and outputs follow structured, schema-validated data standards, making every result AI-ready. Q3 is open source under Apache 2.0 and built to be reused, extended, and improved by the research community. Q3 points to a paradigm shift in how computational quantum science is done. Scientists define the physics, formulate the models, and set the criteria for validation; AI agents write, test, and optimize the code. The decades-long path from a new theory to a trusted, high-performance implementation can thus collapse to days, significantly accelerating scientific discovery.

</details>
