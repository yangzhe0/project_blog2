### 1. TASK Memory: Beyond Text-Only Memory with Activation Steering and Knowledge Editing against Memory Hallucination

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34762v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-25
- **作者/机构**：Jinkun Zhao, Wenjun Wu
- **主题标签**：`RAG`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI

#### 一句话结论
TASK memory 用文本笔记、激活引导与知识编辑共享模型内地址，针对“使用时记忆幻觉”在冻结模型上显著降低忽略、误用和版本混淆。

#### 核心内容
- 定义：正确记忆已检索进提示，模型仍答错或用错题，源于参数知识与外部记忆冲突。
- 三类：忽略记忆最高46%；误用记忆最高24%（检索加权重编辑时49%）；版本混淆中53%仍答被取代值。
- 方法：Text note 存内容；可移除知识编辑以 subject key 供地址并支持多跳；steering vectors 逐查询决定用记忆或模型答案且不存内容。

#### 方法与数据
- 五个开放模型、冻结模型、预注册 test-once；十个模型-数据集单元、300/3000记忆库、CounterFact、MQuAKE，对比 BM25 RAG 与三种 context-steering。
- 忽略降至≤1.5%；邻居误用39.2%→7.5%，CounterFact +10.8pp；300记忆较BM25 RAG +16.4pp且3000保持；无需fact IDs解决修订；MQuAKE多跳+7.5pp；23项跨模型比较21项显著。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：细分并量化记忆幻觉，多模型多数据集改进显著。
- **可复用点**：内容/地址分离、可移除 subject key、逐查询 steering 与 test-once 协议。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未明确计算/存储开销、更大或非开放模型、真实长期动态记忆表现。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
LLM agents increasingly rely on external memory, yet they still hallucinate about what they remember. Often the correct memory is retrieved and placed in the prompt, but the answer contradicts it or the memory is applied to the wrong question. We call these use-time memory hallucinations. Across five open models, we find that they arise from the conflict between parametric knowledge and external memory and take three forms: memory-ignoring, where the model returns to a stale fact in its weights (up to 46% of queries); memory-misapplying, where it attributes the memory to unrelated entities (up to 24%, and 49% when retrieval is combined with weight editing); and version confusion, where 53% of answers about revised facts assert the superseded value when both versions remain stored. We propose TASK memory, which goes beyond text-only memory by combining a Text note with Activation Steering and Knowledge editing that share one in-model address. The text holds the content. A removable knowledge edit supplies the address through its subject key and supports multi-hop composition. A pair of steering vectors decides, query by query, whether the model adopts the memory or keeps its own answer; the vectors store no content and control how the text memory is used. On frozen models under a pre-registered, test-once protocol, TASK reduces memory-ignoring hallucinations to at most 1.5% in all ten model-dataset cells and cuts neighbour misuse from 39.2% to 7.5% (+10.8 pp composite on CounterFact). It improves a 300-memory library over BM25 RAG by 16.4 pp, an advantage that holds at 3,000 memories, resolves fact revisions without fact IDs, and adds 7.5 pp of multi-hop accuracy on MQuAKE. 21 of 23 cross-model comparisons (8B-27B, four families) are significant, and TASK beats three recent context-steering methods in 9 of 10 model-dataset cells.

### 2. AutoRSP: An AI Agent for Radar Sensing Pipeline Design

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34754v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-25
- **作者/机构**：Zhi Zheng, Xincheng Xie, Avery Gump, Sacha Jungerman, Hongyang Li, Yin Li
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.NI, cs.MA, eess.SP

#### 一句话结论
AutoRSP 是一个可自动设计雷达感知流水线的 AI 智能体，能用分层子智能体和真实反馈生成可执行方案，性能达到或超过人类专家与闭源智能体。

#### 核心内容
- 目标是探索 AI 智能体能否以更少时间、成本和专业知识完成跨硬件与软件的雷达流水线设计。
- 它将雷达系统结构嵌入映射到五个栈层的子智能体，并用代码表示各层及显式跨层依赖。
- 在机器人成像、汽车感知和生命体征感知中，生成的流水线性能与专家或闭源智能体相当或更优。

#### 方法与数据
- 智能体搜索反馈来自真实射频仿真、传感器仿真和真实边缘设备执行，而非 LLM 裁判；并提出了高效优化智能体循环的方法。
- 摘要未明确具体数据集、硬件型号与量化结果。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：用真实执行反馈替代 LLM 评判来自动设计雷达全栈流水线。
- **可复用点**：五层栈映射、代码化表示、跨层依赖建模及仿真到边缘设备的多级反馈。
- **局限/待核查**：具体性能数值、成本节省与泛化边界未在摘要中给出；测试框架、工具与基准将发布。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Radar sensing demands a pipeline that is well-thought-out across hardware and software. We ask whether an AI agent can automatically design radar sensing pipelines at a fraction of the time, cost, and expertise that current practice needs. We present AutoRSP, an agent that embeds radar-system-level structure through sub-agents mapped to five stack layers, a code-based representation of every layer with explicitly modeled cross-layer dependencies. Feedback for the agentic search process comes from a pipeline of realistic RF simulation, on-sensor emulation, and execution on real edge devices, rather than from an LLM judge. Because such feedback can be slower than an LLM call, we present methods to efficiently optimize agentic loops. We evaluate AutoRSP on robotic imaging, automotive perception, and vital sign sensing. It produces executable pipelines with performance comparable to or better than human-expert pipelines and closed-source agents. AutoRSP's harness, tools, and benchmarks will be released to bridge the rising gap in agentic research abilities outside of frontier labs.

</details>
