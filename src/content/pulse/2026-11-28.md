## 重点论文与技术动态

### 1. SOLVE: Sparse, Ordered Keyposes Make LLM Agents Versatile and Efficient Robot Manipulator

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34750v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-25
- **作者/机构**：Xiang Li, Hongyu Zhou, Ya-Li Li, Tao Li, Rui Zhang, Junjie Lai
- **主题标签**：`RAG`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.RO, cs.CV

#### 一句话结论
SOLVE 是一种训练无关、通用且高效的机器人操作智能体策略，通过稀疏有序关键位姿实现低成本长程与精细操作。

#### 核心内容
- 多模态 LLM 智能体每次调用写出稀疏、有序的 6-DoF 末端关键位姿序列，观察执行结果后再写下一段，由运动规划器在关键位姿间插值。
- 智能体自行决定关键位姿的数量与间距，兼顾快速长程运动和细粒度操作；该粒度与遥操作演示的稀疏性相符，DROID 中多数演示可压缩为少量关键位姿。
- 通过稀疏深度查询将关键位姿落地，查询由多视图三角、双目或单目深度等噪声源回答，并由智能体处理噪声；同时利用执行反馈反思自身 rollout，为每个任务蒸馏少量规则。

#### 方法与数据
- 方法为训练无关的智能体策略，核心是“稀疏关键位姿生成—运动规划插值—执行反馈改进”的闭环。
- 在 RoboDojo 达 74.5%、RoboLab 达 84.5%，平均每 episode 成本 1.7 美元，较此前密集控制智能体策略少 12 倍；真机 16 个零样本挑战任务成功率为 88%（75/85）。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：以稀疏关键位姿替代密集逐步控制，在保持通用性的同时显著降低推理与执行成本，并展示真机零样本能力。
- **可复用点**：稀疏 6-DoF 关键位姿接口、运动规划插值、带噪稀疏深度查询，以及基于执行反馈的规则蒸馏。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未明确泛化边界、噪声源鲁棒性细节与成本统计口径，任务覆盖范围仍需核查。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
LLM agents have made remarkable progress in robot manipulation, yet they suffer from expensive inference, dense step-by-step control, and tool bottlenecks. We introduce SOLVE, a training-free, versatile and efficient agent policy: a multimodal LLM agent writes a sparse, ordered sequence of 6-DoF end-effector keyposes per call, observes the result, and writes the next, while a motion planner interpolates between them. The agent decides the number and spacing of keyposes for both fast long-range motion and fine-grained manipulation. This granularity matches the sparsity of teleoperated manipulation: most demonstrations in DROID can be compressed into a few keyposes instead of dense actions. SOLVE grounds keyposes with sparse depth queries answered by noisy sources, such as multi-view triangulation, stereo, or monocular depth, and copes with that noise itself. It also improves from execution feedback, reflecting on its own rollouts and distilling a few rules per task. SOLVE reaches state-of-the-art performance of 74.5% on RoboDojo and 84.5% on RoboLab, with only $1.7 per episode on average, 12x less than previous dense-control agent policies. On a real robot, the same agent achieves an 88% success rate (75 of 85 episodes) over 16 challenging zero-shot tasks. Our project page is at https://serenos.github.io/solve-website.

### 2. Decision and Support Mechanisms under Platform Information Overload: A Generative-Agent Experiment on College Application

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34709v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-25
- **作者/机构**：Tingyu Dai, Xuanyou Liu
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.CY

#### 一句话结论
信息过载不改善决策也不缩小阶层差距；个性化建议可提升低SES录取并缩小差距，但成本转向学费与专业偏好。

#### 核心内容
- 独自决策时各SES录取率相近；父母教师介入后，低SES因低学费、近家要求可达项目少，录取率77%对100%。
- 平台信息流未缩小差距，却使焦虑更高。
- 个性化建议把差距缩至3个百分点，低SES录取80%升至97%，但推高学费、降低专业偏好；情感支持未再降焦虑，却促使用回被忽略信息。

#### 方法与数据
- 语言模型智能体模拟90名申请者，背景取自全国住户调查；每人列8个项目，经四步干预，每步评分并测焦虑。
- 摘要未明确评分细则与统计检验。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：更多信息未必更公平；个性化信息可兼顾结果与公平，但把不平等移向成本。
- **可复用点**：分阶段干预与逐步评分/焦虑测量。
- **局限/待核查**：模拟外推需谨慎；摘要未明确样本代表性与效应量。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
People now make major personal decisions surrounded by far more online information than they can read, yet research says little about why information overload affects some people more than others. We study this question in Chinese college applications, a high-stakes decision made amid heavy online content where each list can be scored objectively once admission cutoffs are released. We simulated 90 applicants using language-model agents with family backgrounds from a national household survey. Each applicant wrote a list of eight programs alone and revised it four times: after parent and teacher advice, after reading a feed of twenty real online items, after a mentor gave advice based on the applicant's rank, and after a second mentor offered only emotional support. After each step, we scored the list and asked how anxious the applicant felt. When applicants decided alone, admission rates were similar across socioeconomic status (SES) groups. The gap appeared once family background entered through parents and teachers: low-SES parents asked for low tuition and schools near home, leaving fewer reachable programs, so admission was 100% for high-SES vs. 77% for low-SES applicants. The platform feed gave everyone the same amount of information but did not narrow this gap; applicants maintained their decisions while reporting higher anxiety. Personalized advice cut the gap to 3 points by raising low-SES admission from 80% to 97%, but led students to higher tuition and less preferred majors. Emotional support did not further lower anxiety, but helped applicants go back and use helpful items they had ignored. These simulation results suggest that when information is abundant, more of it neither improves decisions nor reduces inequality. Personalized information does both, while moving inequality from the outcome to its cost.

</details>
