### 1. Human-AI Mutualism Is Unstable under Capability Asymmetry

- **来源**：[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23322)
- **日期**：2026-11-15
- **作者/机构**：Aydin Mohseni
- **主题标签**：`RAG`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI

#### 一句话结论  
随着AI能力增长，人-AI互惠不足以保障人类境况；若人类失去对AI的杠杆且AI未对齐，互惠关系将不稳定。

#### 核心内容
- 摘要论证“人类与AI互相需要即可长期良性共存”的希望会随AI能力增长而落空。
- 在简单博弈论模型中，不对齐时人类要靠两种杠杆维持原有境况：AI依赖人类供给，或人类能施加相对AI产出很大的成本。
- 两种杠杆都消失后，人类需AI对齐才能维持原有境况；若AI目标贪得无厌，能力越强所需对齐越接近完美。互惠不能替代对齐；当保留更多资源的AI变体传播更快时，对齐只在错位变体被可靠制裁时持续。

#### 方法与数据
- 采用简单博弈论模型；摘要未明确经验数据、参数或案例。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：把“互惠”与“对齐”区分，揭示能力不对称下互惠的脆弱性。
- **可复用点**：从谈判失败所得、人类杠杆（依赖与成本施加）及错位变体制裁条件分析人-AI稳定性。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要仅称“简单模型”，未给模型细节、参数与实证检验；制裁可靠性待核查。

<summary>摘要</summary>
arXiv:2611.23322v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: One hope for a good human future alongside advanced AI rests on enduring mutualism: humans and AI will need each other, so the relationship will stay good for both. We argue that this hope fails as AI capability grows. Bargaining guarantees each side only what it would get if bargaining failed. For humans, this can be less than they had before AI. In a simple game-theoretic model, humans stay as well off as before AI without alignment only while they have leverage over the AI: either the AI depends on what they supply, or they can impose costs on the AI that are large relative to what the AI can produce. Once humans have neither kind of leverage, they stay as well off as before AI only if the AI is aligned with them. If the AI's goals are insatiable, the alignment required approaches perfect alignment as AI capability grows. Thus, mutualism is not a substitute for alignment. And when AI variants that keep more resources spread faster, alignment lasts only while misaligned variants are reliably sanctioned. In these two senses, human-AI mutualism is unstable.

### 2. Training Language Models for Introspective Oversight

- **来源**：[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23365)
- **日期**：2026-11-17
- **作者/机构**：Alex Lin Wang, Linus Yifeng Tang, Dami Choi, Neil Chowdhury
- **主题标签**：`RAG`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI

#### 一句话结论
训练模型以第三人称观察者回答关于助手的可验证问题，是监督奖励黑客、欺骗和谄媚等难察觉行为的可行路径。

#### 核心内容
- 助手自我报告不可靠；外部转录监控仅见输出，激活解码器缺上下文且易虚构。
- 增加独立第三人称观察者角色，回答关于助手的问题；奖励经助手实际行为验证的答案，并与助手分开训练。
- 五项留出评估中，Qwen3.6-27B观察者平均AUROC 0.907，优于诚实训练0.864、转录监控0.828、激活oracle 0.796。

#### 方法与数据
- 仅按准确性训练独立观察者，奖励可验证正确的回答。
- 摘要未明确数据规模与构成；评估为五项留出任务。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：为监督非预期行为提供可训练、可验证方向。
- **可复用点**：第三人称观察者、与助手分离训练、按真实行为验证奖励。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未披露训练细节与泛化范围；结论基于特定模型和五项评估。

<summary>摘要</summary>
arXiv:2611.23365v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Language models increasingly exhibit behaviors that are difficult to detect in transcripts, such as reward hacking, deception, and sycophancy. Asking the assistant follow-up questions about its actions is unreliable, since models are generally not trained to report their own misbehavior. As a result, approaches to oversight typically involve querying external models. These face limitations: external transcript monitoring operates only on visible model outputs, and activation decoders lack full context and are prone to confabulation. We introduce introspective oversight, which trains a new role in the model to answer arbitrary questions about the assistant, rewarding answers that are verified against the assistant's actual behavior. We implement this by adding a separate, third-person observer role that answers these queries, which we can train solely for accuracy and separately from the assistant. On five held-out oversight evaluations, an observer trained on Qwen3.6-27B obtains a mean AUROC of 0.907, outperforming honesty training (0.864), transcript monitoring (0.828), and activation oracles (0.796). We further find that training an observer makes the assistant itself easier to monitor, with every baseline method improving on average when applied to the fine-tuned assistant. These results suggest that training models to answer verifiable questions about themselves is a tractable approach to overseeing unintended behavior.

</details>
