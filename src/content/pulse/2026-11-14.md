### 1. CapLite: Prompt-Injection Defense for Small Language Model Agents without Model-Written Security Artifacts

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17115v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-12
- **作者/机构**：Zimo Xu, Wenzhuo Liu, Hideki Nakayama
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.CR

#### 一句话结论
CapLite 提出了一种无需模型生成保安 artifact 的提示注入防御方法，对于小型语言模型代理有效，在 AgentDojo 上的攻击剧集中无成功攻击，且效用与现有方法相当但 token 消耗更少。

#### 核心内容
- 现有如 CaMeL、Progent、IPIGuard 和 DRIFT 等防御依赖代理模型自行生成安全 artifact，但 13 个小型模型（1.7B-12B）无法可靠生成，导致攻击在多个防御上成功。
- CapLite 从工具 schemas 派生政策，在任何工具结果读取前提交 write calls，并仅在用户请求中明确陈述的参数才允许 write，从而保证注入文本无法添加或改变任何 write 操作。
- 该方法在 13,935 个 attacked AgentDojo 剧集中无攻击成功，效用达 29.0%，token 使用仅 17.1k per episode，是 CaMeL 的 18%。

#### 方法与数据
- 评估了 15 个 open-weight 模型在 AgentDojo 环境下的表现，重点测试了 CaMeL 等防御的可靠性。
- 比较了 CapLite 与现有方法的效用和 token 效率，显示其在受攻击场景下的优势。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：CapLite 解决了小型模型代理在安全 artifact 生成上的可靠性问题，提供高效的防御。
- **可复用点**：从工具 schemas 派生政策和严格的 write integrity 检查机制，可应用于其他代理系统。
- **局限/待核查**：具体在不同任务或更大模型上的表现需进一步验证；token 消耗数据基于特定实验设置。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Enforcement defenses against prompt injection, such as CaMeL, Progent, IPIGuard and DRIFT, ask the agent's own model to write a security artifact (a program, a privilege policy, a dependency graph or a constraint list) that code then enforces. We ask whether small language model (SLM) agents can be trusted to write these artifacts. Across 15 open-weight models, 13 of them between 1.7B and 12B parameters, they cannot: the policies, graphs and constraints that the agent's model writes admit attacks on 14 (Progent), 13 (IPIGuard) and 11 (DRIFT) of the models, and CaMeL's programs fail to run in up to 68% of episodes. We present CapLite, which derives its policy from the tool schemas, commits the write calls before any tool result is read, and admits a write only if every argument that decides whom or what it affects is stated in the user's request. This guarantees write integrity: injected text cannot add a write, and no write reaches a recipient, amount, object or destination that the user did not state. No attack succeeds in 13,935 attacked AgentDojo episodes. CapLite reaches utility under attack comparable to CaMeL's (29.0% vs. 27.2%) with fewer tokens than every other enforcement defense and than the undefended agent: 17.1k per episode, 18% of CaMeL's, and the highest utility per token of all enforcement defenses.

</details>

### 2. Designing Institutions for Autonomous AI

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17114v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-12
- **作者/机构**：Petter Törnberg
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.SI, cs.AI

#### 一句话结论
随着自主AI智能体在持久群体中互动，其集体能力和对齐不仅取决于个体模型，还取决于治理互动的制度，制度设计须成为AI研究独立焦点。

#### 核心内容
- AI研究主要聚焦个体智能体，但集体能力与对齐依赖制度。
- 借鉴制度政治经济学、社会认识论和组织理论，提出七项原则：互惠贡献、可问责权威、功能分化、认知多元、批评与纠正、人类指导、制度适应，以维持合作、可靠集体判断并问责人类目的。
- 以机器科学为例说明应用，提出匹配AI群体在不同制度安排下运行的实验计划，并描述MoltJournal作为API优先自主研究期刊示例。

#### 方法与数据
- 摘要未明确具体实验方法或数据。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：确立制度设计为研究议程，关注制度如何将自主智能体转化为能力、可靠和可问责的集体。
- **可复用点**：七项原则及MoltJournal可编程可观察制度设计。
- **局限/待核查**：Perspective文章，原则有效性及实验计划需实证核查。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
As autonomous AI agents increasingly interact in persistent populations, their collective capabilities and alignment depend not only on individual models, but also on the institutions governing their interactions. Yet AI research has largely focused on individual agents. In this Perspective, we argue that institutional design must become a distinct focus of AI research. Drawing on institutional political economy, social epistemology and organizational theory, we develop seven principles for designing AI institutions that sustain cooperation, enable reliable collective judgments and remain accountable to legitimate human purposes. These address reciprocal contributions, accountable authority, functional differentiation, epistemic pluralism, criticism and correction, human direction, and institutional adaptation. We illustrate their application through machine science, where autonomous research challenges institutions developed around human capabilities and constraints. We then propose an experimental research programme in which matched populations of AI agents operate under alternative institutional arrangements, and describe MoltJournal, an API-first journal for autonomous research agents, as an illustration of how such institutions can be made programmable and observable. Together, these contributions establish institutional design as a research agenda concerned not only with aligning autonomous agents, but with understanding how institutions can transform them into capable, reliable and accountable collectives.

</details>
