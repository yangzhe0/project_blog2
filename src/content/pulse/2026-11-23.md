### 1. Adding One More Agent: How AI Team Size and Formation Shape Productivity and Diversity in Human-AI Brainstorming Teams

- **来源**：[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26663)
- **日期**：2026-11-19
- **作者/机构**：Rong-Ching Chang, Hao-Chuan Wang
- **主题标签**：`RAG`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI

#### 一句话结论
头脑风暴实验中，增加第二个AI伙伴虽提高共享板发帖量，却显著减少人类作者想法；单AI下“平均校准”优势在双AI时消失，说明AI团队规模与形成方式会重塑人类贡献，单代理结论不能直接推广。

#### 核心内容
- 双AI房间共享板想法更多，但人类作者想法大幅下降；单AI配平均校准AI时人类发帖最多，双AI时该效应消失。
- 人类仍持续交谈，却更少把想法转化为板上帖子；AI社交性谈话预测更多人类发帖，AI发帖量预测更少。
- AI在场提升人类作者想法的全局多样性，但只有第二个AI增加会话内差异化。

#### 方法与数据
- 头脑风暴实验（N=168），交叉“1/2个AI伙伴”与“平均校准/参与者定制”两种形成方式，以人类-人类双人组为参考。
- 摘要未明确具体任务流程、测量细节与统计方法。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：多AI团队并非简单增益，可能以人类贡献下降为代价。
- **可复用点**：将AI伙伴数量与行为形成作为实验因子，并区分人类发言与板上发帖转化。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未报告效应量、显著性细节及外部效度，具体机制需核查。

arXiv:2611.26663v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Generative AI has improved individual productivity, but whether adding a plurality of AI partners helps or hurts remains open. We crossed AI partnership (one versus two) with behavioral formation (calibrated to average human behavior versus customized by the participant) in a brainstorming experiment ($N$=168) with human-human dyads as reference. Two-AI rooms posted more ideas to the shared board, but human-authored ideas dropped substantially. With one AI partner, formation mattered: humans paired with an average-calibrated AI posted more than any other condition. With two, this effect vanished. Humans continued talking but converted fewer ideas into board posts. AI social talk predicted more human posts, while AI posting volume predicted fewer. AI presence increased global diversity of human-authored ideas, but only a second AI increased within-session differentiation. The number of AI partners and their formation can reshape what the human contributes; single-agent results do not generalize to multi-agent teams.

### 2. Coherence by Construction: Synthesizing CTI Knowledge Graphs and the Formal Limits of Coherence Validation

- **来源**：[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26717)
- **日期**：2026-11-19
- **作者/机构**：Tanzimul Alam Fahim, Mohammad Arif Hossain
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AI

#### 一句话结论
论文提出“模拟生成”的STIX 2.1 CTI知识图谱构造范式，使一致性成为构造属性，并警示常见图特征验证器可能无信息量。

#### 核心内容
- 针对LLM逐对象生成致关系未落地，改为模拟因果过程，只在事件发生时输出关系。
- 在STIX 2.1实例化三种引擎；进化引擎用LLM遗传算子操作结构化图基因组，共享确定性编译器排除畸形/悬空输出。
- 证明引用闭合、因果落地与标识符稳定；消融定位到referee与世界模型，两个LLM评审复现引擎排名。

#### 方法与数据
- 智能体提出意图，确定性referee依据显式世界模型裁决；数据规模与来源摘要未明确。
- 用Erdős–Rényi对照四次检验：结构分离、嵌入迁移、归纳链接预测、真实数据增强。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：提出coherence-by-construction及验证极限定理，对CTI图谱合成与评测有警示。
- **可复用点**：确定性编译器、referee+世界模型约束、随机图对照检验验证器。
- **局限/待核查**：有效benchmark条件与实验细节摘要未明确，需核查泛化性。

<summary>摘要</summary>
arXiv:2611.26717v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Synthesizing relational training data such as knowledge graphs with a large language model typically emits structured objects one at a time, which keeps each object valid but leaves the relationships between them ungrounded. We study an alternative paradigm, generation by simulation: rather than describing a relational artifact, we simulate a process that produces it and take the artifact to be its causal record. Language-model agents propose intent and a deterministic referee resolves outcomes against an explicit world model, so a relation is emitted only when the event it represents occurs, making coherence a property of construction. We instantiate the paradigm for cyber threat intelligence knowledge graphs in the STIX 2.1 standard with three engines, among them an evolutionary engine applying language-model genetic operators to structured graph genomes, sharing a deterministic compiler that makes malformed and dangling output unrepresentable. We prove referential closure, causal grounding, and identifier stability, an ablation localizes each to the referee and world model rather than the policy, and two independent LLM judges reproduce the rubric's engine ranking. Our central result is cautionary, and we prove it: a validator reading any graph feature set a structure-matched random graph can reproduce is uninformative about coherence for a coherence-by-construction generator. Using Erd\H{o}s-R\'enyi controls we realize the theorem four times, as structural separation, embedding transfer, inductive link prediction, and real-data augmentation each reward a triviality rather than coherence; we give the mechanism behind each and the conditions a valid benchmark must meet.
