### 1. From Seed to BLOOM: Autonomously Growing a Better Out-of-Order Core

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26517v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-19
- **作者/机构**：Angela Cui, Ferran Hermida-Rivera, Jack Toubes, Jim Fang, Chengyi Lux Zhang, Borivoje Nikolić
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.AR

#### 一句话结论
MiracleGro 是无需人工干预的 agentic AI 系统，可在 BOOM 乱序核上自动分析周期级 RTL 与综合结果、定位瓶颈并实现 RTL 改进，显著提升性能与频率。

#### 核心内容
- 微处理器性能优化需兼顾面积、频率等 ASIC 约束，并跨架构、设计与性能分析，传统上劳动密集。
- MiracleGro 在开源乱序超标量应用核 BOOM 上自动完成周期级 RTL 行为与综合结果分析、瓶颈识别及架构想法到 RTL 的转换。
- 考虑周期精确性能、面积和关键路径后，自动生成改进设计：SPEC CPU2017 全参考输入上 IPC 提升 85%，SkyWater 130nm 频率提升 27%。

#### 方法与数据
- 方法为 agentic AI 系统，自动分析周期级 RTL 行为与综合结果、识别瓶颈并实现 RTL 改进；摘要未明确具体模型与训练细节。
- 在 BOOM 与 SPEC CPU2017 上评估；完整 intspeed（30 万亿指令）墙钟加速 2.34 倍。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：展示前沿模型自动改进 RTL 驱动平台、开展实现导向微架构研究的潜力。
- **可复用点**：将周期精确性能、面积、关键路径纳入自动分析—瓶颈—RTL 实现闭环。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未明确通用性、其他工艺/设计适用性及验证开销与可复现细节。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Improving microprocessor performance while meeting ASIC implementation constraints like area and frequency is a complex and labor-intensive task that requires expertise across computer architecture, hardware design, and performance analysis and profiling. While large teams in industry can afford the engineering resources to target this problem across performance modeling, RTL design, and ASIC implementations, small teams of researchers and engineers cannot take on this huge engineering burden while keeping up with the pace of advancement in processor microarchitecture. Recent advancements in large language model (LLM) agents present a promising substrate to accelerate this traditionally human-intensive process. In this work, we build and evaluate MiracleGro, an agentic AI-based system that, without human intervention, analyzes cycle-level RTL behavior and synthesis results, identifies performance bottlenecks, and translates architectural ideas into RTL implementation in a leading open-source out-of-order superscalar application core, the Berkeley Out-of-Order Machine (BOOM). Taking into account cycle-exact performance, area, and critical path, MiracleGro automatically produces improved BOOM designs that drastically increase the human-optimized BOOM's performance on substantial workload suites such as SPEC CPU2017 with full reference inputs. MiracleGro achieves an 85% IPC speedup and a frequency improvement of 27% in SkyWater 130nm, resulting in a 2.34x wall-clock time speedup on the full SPEC CPU2017 intspeed benchmarks (30 trillion instructions). This result demonstrates the enormous opportunity to harness frontier models to improve state-of-the-art RTL-driven platforms for implementation-grounded microarchitecture research across hardware designs.

</details>

### 2. AutoMaster: Autonomous Post-Training by Making the Same Model a Better Teacher

- **来源**：[arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26510v1)
- **日期**：2026-11-19
- **作者/机构**：Guoxin Chen, Shuang Sun, Fanzhe Meng, Xinyu Tang, Ruihua Song, Wayne Xin Zhao
- **主题标签**：`AI-Agent`, `arXiv`
- **推荐等级**：高
- **分类**：cs.CL

#### 一句话结论
AutoMaster用自然语言技能干预教师轨迹再微调目标模型，在固定教师、任务池、训练集规模和SFT配方下优于Vanilla SFT。

#### 核心内容
- 任务成功不能说明应强化哪些行为；成功教师轨迹仍可能含无依据假设、无效重复或表面验证。
- Skill-FT用自然语言技能抑制不良习惯、激发教师rollout中代表性不足的有效行为，再微调目标模型。
- AutoMaster是自主后训练系统，LLM agent通过轨迹分析、技能设计、数据选择和评估反馈开发干预。

#### 方法与数据
- 在Terminal、Search和软件工程任务中，用4B和9B目标模型；相对成功但未引导教师轨迹的Vanilla SFT，平均性能提升1.42-8.99个百分点，且教师、任务池、训练集规模和SFT配方固定。
- 六种设置中SFT保留技能指令比移除的平均性能更高，尽管评估时无技能指令且监督输出相同；具体数据规模摘要未明确。

#### 价值判断
- **值得关注**：行为塑造可使固定模型成为更有效教师。
- **可复用点**：自然语言技能干预、轨迹分析、数据选择与反馈闭环。
- **局限/待核查**：摘要未明确任务池规模、评测细节及跨任务泛化性。

<details>
<summary>摘要</summary>
Task success alone does not specify which behaviors post-training should reinforce. Even successful teacher trajectories can contain unsupported assumptions, unproductive repetition, or superficial verification. We propose Skill-FT (Skill-Guided Fine-Tuning), which uses natural-language skills to suppress undesirable habits and elicit underrepresented effective behaviors during teacher rollouts, then fine-tunes a target model on the resulting trajectories. We introduce AutoMaster, an autonomous post-training system in which an LLM agent develops these interventions through trajectory analysis, skill design, data selection, and feedback from rollouts and target-model evaluation. Across Terminal, Search, and software engineering tasks with 4B and 9B target models, AutoMaster improves mean performance over Vanilla SFT on successful unguided teacher trajectories by 1.42-8.99 percentage points, with the teacher, task pool, training set size, and SFT recipe held fixed. Together with the ablations and trajectory analyses, these results suggest that behavioral shaping can make a fixed model a more effective teacher. We further find that retaining skill instructions during SFT yields higher mean performance than removing them across all six settings, despite their absence at evaluation and identical supervised outputs. These findings suggest that preserving skill context as part of a training demonstration can support better behavioral internalization.

</details>
